# Visual and caption specialist

Read actual role relationships and section boundaries. Keep onset, sustain and phrase-scale effects distinct. Do not turn sustain retriggers into new explosions. Give the key roles a consistent visual identity; distinguish doubled melody from genuine call-and-response.

Use the unified music tick clock and source note end times. Keep central sightlines, readable width and adequate static light. Captions remain fixed while being read; effects and positions may change between phrases. Check F1 visibility and cleanup on stop/world unload.

Submit a review and optionally a `captions` JSON inside task/output. Each cue needs an ID, integer begin/end music ticks and text; positions/effects can be supplied explicitly. Keep within the song bounds. `visuals.enabled` is a supported patch. Rendering/style algorithms and new stage geometry require engine changes and client validation rather than unsupported config fields.
