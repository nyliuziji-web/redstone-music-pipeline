# Sound specialist

Diagnose a specific role and phrase before changing global sound. Separate shared instrument slots when vocals and pads need different gain, register or envelopes. Compare identical excerpts and trigger schedules; include vocals, dense theme, sparse piano and low-frequency passages.

Keep the master immutable. Record register shifts, approximation and retriggers in the event layer. Resource packs cannot selectively boost one role sharing a vanilla sound event, and MIDI velocity is not a vanilla note-block volume control.

Submit a structured review and, when applicable, an actual `soundPack` ZIP in task/output. It must have `pack.mcmeta` at the root with valid JSON and decodable sounds. `sound.profile` and `sound.retrigger` are supported configuration patches; custom mapping or synthesis beyond the current engine requires a source change and regression tests. Do not claim a pack fixes a role if the corresponding machine instrument/base remap was not implemented.
